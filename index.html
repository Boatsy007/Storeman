COMMON QUESTIONS

GOOD TO
KNOW.

Quick answers before we arrange your free site assessment.

Do you offer recurring maintenance?+

Yes. Storeman is built for both one-off work and recurring commercial maintenance. We can discuss weekly, fortnightly, monthly or a schedule suited to your property.

What areas do you service?+

We service the Gold Coast, Yatala, Logan, Brisbane and surrounding areas across South East Queensland.

Can you combine multiple services?+

Yes. You can combine sweeping, grounds maintenance, lawn and garden, pressure washing and fleet washing so more of the exterior is handled by one contractor.

Do you work outside business hours?+

Where site access and scheduling allow, we can discuss work outside normal operating hours to reduce disruption. We’ll confirm what is practical during quoting.

How does the free quote work?+

Send us your details and the services you need. Storeman will contact you to arrange a free site assessment, confirm the scope of work and then prepare your quote.

COMMON QUESTIONS

GOOD TO
KNOW.

Quick answers before you request your free quote.

Do you service both homes and businesses?+

Yes. STOREMAN provides lawn and grounds care for residential homes, rental properties, businesses, strata, body corporate and other managed properties.

What is included in a standard lawn service?+

A standard visit follows the STOREMAN sequence: mow, snip, edge, then blow and tidy surrounding hard surfaces before we leave.

What is the minimum price?+

Our minimum standard visit price is $100. Larger or more involved properties are quoted higher based on the work required.

How does the lawn membership work?+

The membership includes 19 scheduled visits each year: 13 fortnightly visits during the six-month summer growth period and 6 monthly visits during the six-month winter period. Members save 10% compared with booking those same visits individually.

Do you offer recurring commercial grounds maintenance?+

Yes. We quote recurring mowing and grounds maintenance for businesses, strata, body corporate and managed properties.

What areas do you service?+

We service the Gold Coast, Logan, Brisbane and surrounding South East Queensland areas, depending on route availability and property scope.

How does the free instant quote work?+

Enter your property details, choose any extras and upload current photos. Standard properties can receive an instant quote, while larger or unusual properties are sent for review.

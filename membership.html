HOW THE YEAR WORKS

MORE OFTEN WHEN THE GRASS GROWS. LESS OFTEN WHEN IT SLOWS.

The membership is structured around the seasons. During the six-month high-growth period, your lawn is serviced fortnightly. During the six-month slower-growth period, servicing moves to monthly.

The result is 19 scheduled visits each year without the need to keep rebooking every time the lawn gets long.

MEMBERSHIP QUESTIONS

GOOD TO
KNOW.

Is Lawn Green-Up included?

Yes. Storeman Lawn Green-Up is included as part of every standard scheduled lawn visit at no extra charge.

Is the 10% discount based on my quoted lawn price?

Yes. Your property is quoted first. The annual membership price is then calculated from your normal per-visit price across 19 visits, less the 10% member saving.

Can I choose how I pay?

Yes. The annual membership can be spread across weekly, fortnightly or monthly payments. Your service schedule stays the same regardless of which payment frequency you choose.

Does every lawn cost the same?

No. $100 is the minimum standard visit price. Larger lawns, difficult access or properties requiring more time are quoted higher before you join.

Can I still book without becoming a member?

Yes. The membership is an option for customers who want recurring scheduled care. One-off and other recurring lawn services can still be quoted separately.
